05 — Usage
Do & Don't
Guidelines for consistent, correct use of the KTestify identity.
✓ Do
  • Use the icon on dark backgrounds (#0C1018 or darker)
  • Keep the K accent in mint (#6EE7B7) in the wordmark
  • Maintain minimum clear space — icon height × 0.25 on all sides
  • Use Space Mono Bold for the wordmark exclusively
  • Scale the icon proportionally from the provided SVG
  • Use the light-bg reversed version on white/light surfaces
✗ Don't
  • Don't recolor the icon accent to non-brand colors
  • Don't stretch or distort the icon — scale uniformly
  • Don't place the dark icon on light backgrounds without the reversed variant
  • Don't separate the K from the icon — the lettermark K uses the same mint
  • Don't use the wordmark below 12px — use icon only at small sizes
  • Don't add drop shadows, outlines, or effects to the wordmark
